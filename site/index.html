Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

A

Alarms that nag you

Set a reminder time and Taskys will pop up over everything with an alarm sound. Snooze it for 5 or 15 minutes — it will keep coming back until you do it.

◐

Focus timer

Built-in customizable Pomodoro. Link it to a specific task and track your focus cycles.

▤

Real statistics

Productivity over time, tasks per project and priority distribution. Your data never leaves your machine.

↻

Recurring tasks

Daily, weekly or monthly habits that automatically reschedule when completed.

P

Projects & subtasks

Organize with projects, break work down into subtasks with visual progress bars.

⌘

Keyboard-first

Ctrl+N new task · Ctrl+F search · Ctrl+Shift+P focus timer.
